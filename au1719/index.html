Why Ownership is on this list. The other four rows are internal hygiene. This one is strategic. McKinsey's test for when data becomes an advantage is four-part: captured as the work happens, structured for reuse, traceable to origin, and held with the rights to keep learning from it. That fourth clause is the one most firms already answered years ago, in a software contract nobody re-read.
